Bulk data with conditions ulcerative colitis (UC) and Crohn’s disease (CD).

The study assesses transcriptional profiles in peripheral blood mononuclear cells from 42 healthy individuals, 59 CD patients, and 26 UC patients.

This data consists of 127 samples × 22283 genes.
